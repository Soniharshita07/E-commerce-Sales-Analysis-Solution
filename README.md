# 🛍️ End-to-End E-Commerce Sales & Seasonality Analysis (2024–2025)

An end-to-end data analysis project investigating multi-year e-commerce performance, seasonal trends, product category mix, and return/cancellation metrics using Python (Pandas & Matplotlib).

---

## 📊 Project Overview
This project processes, cleans, and analyzes transactional data across orders, order items, and products for **2024 and 2025**. By establishing a strict revenue definition (factoring in quantities, unit prices, and discounts for completed orders only), the analysis uncovers key business drivers, customer purchasing patterns, and risk areas like category-specific return rates.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Libraries
* **Language:** Python
* **Data Manipulation & Analysis:** Pandas, NumPy
* **Data Visualization:** Matplotlib, Seaborn

---

## 📈 Key Insights & Findings
1. **Q4 Holiday Surge:** Monthly revenue consistently peaks in November, growing from **100,091.48** in 2024 to **128,450.11** in 2025, highlighting a strong year-end shopping cycle.
2. **Furniture Category Dynamics:** Furniture commands the largest revenue share (**18.0%**) and the highest Average Order Value (**~$360**), but it also suffers from the highest return/cancellation rate (**14.5%**).
3. **Severe Seasonality in Product Mix:** Outdoor products experience massive seasonal swings, rising from **2.19%** of monthly revenue in January up to a peak of **29.70%** in June.

---

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations
* **Scale Inventory Ahead of Q4:** Advance inventory preparation and ramp up early marketing campaigns to maximize the November demand spike[cite: 2, 10].
* **Mitigate Furniture Returns:** Improve product descriptions, dimension guides, and handling quality control for high-value furniture items to protect margins against the **14.5%** return rate.
